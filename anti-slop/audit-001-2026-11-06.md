# Anti-Slop Audit Report 001 (2026-11-06)

**Project:** CampusQuest (Kelompok 5 - Telkom University)
**Mode:** `after` (Audit of existing Stitch-exported Blade templates & codebase)
**Scope:** `resources/views/**`, `public/css/style.css`, `routes/web.php`

---

## Findings List

### HIGH Priority (Hard Gate Rules)

1. **[HIGH] [R-02 — Copywriting (Em Dash Forbidden)]** `resources/views/layouts/app.blade.php`, `resources/views/auth/login.blade.php`, `resources/views/dashboard.blade.php`, `resources/views/leaderboard.blade.php`, `resources/views/missions/index.blade.php`, `resources/views/submissions/index.blade.php`, `resources/views/teams/index.blade.php`, `resources/views/portfolio/index.blade.php`: Page titles, footers, and labels contain the forbidden em dash character (`—`), such as `CampusQuest — Portal Gamifikasi` and `© 2025 CampusQuest — Dikembangkan oleh Tim Kelompok 5`.
2. **[HIGH] [R-03 — Mobile Responsiveness]** `resources/views/layouts/app.blade.php` & `resources/views/admin/submissions.blade.php`: The sidebar (`fixed left-6 w-[280px]` / `fixed left-0 w-72`) and content wrapper (`pl-[328px]` / `pl-72`) are locked to desktop widths without mobile breakpoints (`hidden lg:flex` / `lg:pl-[328px]`) or a mobile drawer toggle, crushing content and causing horizontal overflow on phone screens.
3. **[HIGH] [R-17 & R-36 — Unverified Statistics & Fabricated Claims]** `resources/views/auth/login.blade.php` & `resources/views/portfolio/index.blade.php`: Unverified statistics and fabricated technical claims appear in UI copy (`"3,450+ Mahasiswa telah mengumpulkan lebih dari 128,000 XP"`, `"Enkripsi TLS 256-bit standar Puspresnas & BAAK"`, `"Tervalidasi Otomatis via Blockchain Vault"`, `"SLA 98.2% On-time"`).
4. **[HIGH] [R-23 & R-38 — Assumed AI Avatars, Logo & Fictional Names]** All 8 Blade views: External AI-generated portrait URLs (`lh3.googleusercontent.com/aida-public/...`) and fictional student identities (`Nabila Putri`, `Rian Firmansyah`, `Farhan Maulana`) are hardcoded instead of using the real Kelompok 5 student records from the database (Nathasya, Fathur, Anggita, Nayla, Naira, Syadira) and honest initial avatars.
5. **[HIGH] [R-24 & R-26 — Dead Links & Non-Functional Controls]** `resources/views/auth/login.blade.php`, `resources/views/admin/submissions.blade.php`, `resources/views/layouts/app.blade.php`, `resources/views/submissions/index.blade.php`: Multiple links and controls point to `href="#"` or only run fake client-side alerts (`Panduan SIAKAD`, `Kebijakan Privasi`, `Kontak BAAK`, `Arsip & Riwayat Persetujuan`, `Laporan & Rekap XP`, `Pengaturan Verifikator`, topbar search/notification buttons), and the submission/admin forms are not wired to persist changes to the SQLite database.
6. **[HIGH] [R-25 — WCAG AA Color Contrast Failures]** `resources/views/admin/submissions.blade.php` & `resources/views/auth/login.blade.php`: White text (`#FFFFFF`) on emerald badges/buttons (`#00B894`) has a contrast ratio of **2.54:1** (fails WCAG AA 4.5:1), and `text-slate-400` (`#94A3B8`) on white (`#FFFFFF`) has a contrast ratio of **2.56:1** (fails WCAG AA 4.5:1).
7. **[HIGH] [R-27 — Missing Empty, Loading, and Error States]** `resources/views/dashboard.blade.php`, `resources/views/missions/index.blade.php`, `resources/views/leaderboard.blade.php`, `resources/views/teams/index.blade.php`, `resources/views/portfolio/index.blade.php`: Views only display a hardcoded happy path and lack `@forelse ... @empty` states, form validation `@error` banners, and loading states.
8. **[HIGH] [R-32 & R-39 — Keyboard Focus & Accessible Semantics]** `resources/views/layouts/app.blade.php` & all Blade views: Interactive controls lack global `cursor: pointer`, inputs suppress focus outlines (`outline-none`) without accessible focus rings, icon-only buttons lack `aria-label`, and decorative icons lack `aria-hidden="true"`.
9. **[HIGH] [R-40 — Performance Mechanisms]** `resources/views/layouts/app.blade.php`, `resources/views/auth/login.blade.php`, `resources/views/admin/submissions.blade.php`: `<img>` elements lack explicit `width` and `height` attributes causing layout shift (CLS), and third-party icon/script tags in `<head>` block rendering.

---

### MEDIUM Priority (Purpose-Gate Rules)

10. **[MEDIUM] [R-01 — Default Purple/Violet Gradients Without Written Hierarchy Purpose]** `resources/views/layouts/app.blade.php`, `resources/views/admin/submissions.blade.php`, `resources/views/dashboard.blade.php`: Purple-to-secondary gradients (`bg-gradient-to-r from-primary-container to-secondary`) are used across progress bars, badges, and buttons without a written hierarchy justification in `DESIGN.md`.
11. **[MEDIUM] [R-04 — Generic Sparkle & Lightning Icons]** All Blade views: Generic AI icons (`ph-sparkle`, `stars`, `bolt`) are repeated across headers, pills, and XP counters as decoration without written relevance.
12. **[MEDIUM] [R-09 — Eyebrow Capsule Badges Above Every H1]** `resources/views/auth/login.blade.php`, `resources/views/dashboard.blade.php`, `resources/views/leaderboard.blade.php`, `resources/views/teams/index.blade.php`, `resources/views/portfolio/index.blade.php`: Every page places a small capsule badge directly above the `<h1>` repeating what the heading already states.
13. **[MEDIUM] [R-10 — Excessive Glassmorphism]** `resources/views/layouts/app.blade.php`, `resources/views/auth/login.blade.php`, `resources/views/admin/submissions.blade.php`: `backdrop-blur-xl` / `backdrop-blur-md` is applied across headers and modal overlays simultaneously without an elevation cap.

---

### LOW Priority (Quality Locks & Code Comment Hygiene)

14. **[LOW] [R-11 — Excessive Pill Border Radius]** All Blade views: Nearly every UI element (sidebar links, search inputs, filter tabs, status badges, buttons) uses `rounded-full` simultaneously, reducing shape distinction between inputs, tags, and buttons.
15. **[LOW] [R-31 & R-37 — Missing `DESIGN.md` Direction & Liveliness Dials]** Project root: No `DESIGN.md` file exists to formally record the one-line reasons for visual choices (color, typography, layout, spacing) and the Liveliness Dials (`ENERGY / RHYTHM / MOTION`).
16. **[LOW] [antislop-code — Decorative Banner & Narration Comments]** `routes/web.php`, `public/css/style.css`, and Blade views: Files contain decorative ASCII separator banners (`// =========================================================================`) and step-by-step HTML section narration comments (`<!-- 1. Hero Welcome... -->`, `<!-- Card 1: ... -->`).
